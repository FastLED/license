answer to a normalized question; an authority card records what a reusable

complete source-card registry is the [authority index](authorities/INDEX.md).

## Authority registry

The [authority index](authorities/INDEX.md) lists all reusable cards by stable
`AUTH-####` identifier, title, type, and verification date. Do not infer a
card's proposition or weight from its title; open the card and read its posture,
source quality, actual holding or text, and limits.

## Status key

- Research: `unresearched`, `in-progress`, `partial`, `answered`, or
  `superseded`.
- Legal review: `not-requested`, `pending`, `approved`, or
  `changes-required`.

The verification date reports when time-sensitive sources were last checked;
it is not an attorney approval date.
