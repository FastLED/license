# AUTH-0063: Korean Supreme Court GPL derivative-program trade-secret case

## Citation and source

- Full citation: Supreme Court of Korea, 12 February 2009, 2006도8369 (unfair-competition/trade-secret criminal case).
- Direct primary source: [Korean official case database](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/precInfoR.do?precSeq=129612&vSct=%2A).
- Later history: final Supreme Court judgment.

## Procedural posture and weight

Supreme Court criminal/trade-secret disposition, not a GPL enforcement action.
Original language Korean. No official English translation located; English is a
limited researcher paraphrase. The database anonymises program/party names.

## Claims, relief, and holding

- Licence/version: GNU GPL (version not stated in the official text), VPN application derivative.
- Claim/trigger: defendants challenged a trade-secret conviction; record included refusal to publish derivative source allegedly contrary to GPL condition.
- Requested versus awarded relief: no GPL source-disclosure relief was requested or awarded; Supreme Court dismissed the appeals and left conviction outcome intact.
- Holding: an independently creative derivative's source may remain a trade secret even if the developer's refusal to disclose it breached GPL; liability to the original-program copyright owner for that breach was expressly left as a separate matter.

## What it does not establish

- That GPL breach is permissible, that the source need not be released in an enforcement action, or that the derivative author has rights against the original author.

## Key facts and reasoning

The court separated copyright in an independently creative derivative and the
trade-secret definition from a potential GPL-based claim by the original
author. That separation is the card's narrow use.
