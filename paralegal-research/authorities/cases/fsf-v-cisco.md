# AUTH-0043: *Free Software Foundation, Inc. v. Cisco Systems, Inc.*

## Citation and source
- No. 1:08-cv-10764 (S.D.N.Y.). [Complaint PDF](https://www.fsf.org/licensing/complaint-2008-12-11.pdf/view); [docket identity](https://dockets.justia.com/docket/new-york/nysdce/1%3A2008cv10764/337188).
- Later history/current status: [Settlement announcement](https://softwarefreedom.org/news/2009/may/20/fsf-cisco-settlement/) is a party/representative report, not a court ruling. Obtain docket if terms matter.

## License, claims, requested and actual relief
- License/version and claims: GPLv2 and other FSF-copyrighted programs in Linksys products. FSF alleged copyright infringement from distribution without required source/license materials.
- Requested versus actual relief: Complaint sought infringement remedies, including damages and injunctive relief. Actual relief: publicly announced settlement; reported compliance director/process and undisclosed financial contribution; no merits judgment.

## Procedural posture and precedential weight
Settled federal action; complaint is allegation and settlement is nonprecedential.

## Holding
No court holding establishes breach, source disclosure, or a general GPL remedy.

## Key facts and reasoning
FSF alleged Linksys product distributions included FSF code but did not satisfy GPL obligations.
