# AUTH-0050: *IT Development SAS v Free Mobile SAS*

## Citation and source

- Full citation: Case C-666/18, ECLI:EU:C:2019:1099, judgment of 18 December 2019.
- Direct primary source: [EUR-Lex, French case-language text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/FR/CASE/?uri=CELEX:62018CJ0666); [official English text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX:62018CJ0666).
- Later history/current status: preliminary ruling on a Paris Court of Appeal reference; national remand disposition was not verified in this pass.

## Procedural posture and weight

Binding CJEU preliminary ruling on interpretation of Directives 2004/48 and
2009/24. The CJEU did not decide infringement, damages, or an injunction between
the parties. The French language is the language of the case; the EUR-Lex English
version is an official EU translation, not a researcher translation.

## Claims, relief, and holding

- Licence/version: a paid software licence; no public/open-source licence at issue.
- Claim/trigger: alleged unauthorized source-code modification contrary to licence.
- Requested versus awarded relief: underlying claim sought copyright damages; the CJEU awarded no party-specific relief.
- Holding: a licensee's breach of a software-licence clause concerning an act reserved by Article 4(1) may be treated as infringement where it concerns a limitation within Article 4(2); Article 2 of Directive 2004/48 does not exclude that route.

## What it does not establish

- That every contractual GPL/AGPL term is an Article 4(2) limitation.
- That source disclosure, a commercial licence, damages, or an injunction follows automatically.
- The outcome under French law on remand or in a non-EU forum.

## Key facts and reasoning

IT Development licensed software to Free Mobile and alleged modification beyond
the licence. The Court distinguished the national-law classification question
from the EU rule that the author can invoke Directive 2004/48 remedies when the
licensee infringes a limitation connected with an Article 4 reserved act.
