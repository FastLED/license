# AUTH-0072: *Forest Park Pictures v. Universal Television Network*

## Citation and source

- 683 F.3d 424 (2d Cir. 2012), [published opinion reproduction](https://openjurist.org/683/f3d/424/forest-park-pictures-v-universal-television-network-inc).
- The reproduction is the text of a federal appellate opinion, not a license
  steward statement; verify against an official docket copy before filing.

## Procedural posture and weight

Published appeal from Rule 12(b)(6) dismissal. Binding within the Second
Circuit on its federal preemption analysis and its New York-law pleading
holding, but it does not decide public-license assent.

## Holdings and relief actually awarded

The court vacated dismissal. It held that allegations of an implied promise to
pay for use of a submitted idea adequately pleaded an enforceable New York
contract and supplied an extra element, so the claim was not § 301-preempted.
It did not find a contract after proof or award payment.

## What it supports

- Conduct and surrounding communications can plausibly form an implied-in-fact
  agreement under the selected state law.
- A promise to pay for use can be qualitatively different from exclusive
  copyright rights.

## What it does not establish

- That notice of a public license alone proves assent in New York or elsewhere.
- That every contract claim avoids § 301; the court reserved different facts,
  including a promise duplicating copyright control.
