# AUTH-0044: *Andersen and Landley v. Xterasys Corp.*

## Citation and source
- No. 07-CV-10455 (S.D.N.Y.); [SFLC 2007 archive](https://softwarefreedom.org/news/2007/).
- Later history/current status: Primary court complaint/final order not located in this pass; SFLC announcement is settlement reporting only. Recheck PACER.

## License, claims, requested and actual relief
- License/version and claims: GNU GPLv2 / BusyBox. Developers alleged distribution of BusyBox-containing networking products without complete corresponding source.
- Requested versus actual relief: Complaint reportedly sought copyright remedies and GPL compliance. Actual relief: public settlement report says Xterasys would cease binary distribution pending verification, publish source, notify recipients, appoint compliance officer, and provide undisclosed consideration.

## Procedural posture and precedential weight
Settled action; no precedential merits ruling; full agreement/docket order not reviewed.

## Holding
Settlement terms show negotiated compliance, not what a court necessarily could or did order.

## Key facts and reasoning
SFLC represented BusyBox developers in a 2007 enforcement sweep.
