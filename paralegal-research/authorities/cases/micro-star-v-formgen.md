# AUTH-0077: *Micro Star v. FormGen*

## Citation and source

- 154 F.3d 1107 (9th Cir. 1998), [published opinion reproduction](https://app.midpage.ai/document/micro-star-v-formgen-inc-757702).

## Procedural posture and weight

Published Ninth Circuit preliminary-injunction appeal. Binding in that circuit
on its derivative-work analysis, but it is not a linking or public-license case.

## Holdings and relief actually awarded

The court upheld preliminary injunctive relief against sale of a compilation of
game level files. The files were derivative works because they incorporated
protected story elements through the original game's audiovisual display; an
authorized level editor and user-sharing encouragement did not establish an
unrestricted right to commercially distribute the compilation.

## What it supports

- A digital file can be a derivative work even when it does not contain every
  original asset; the inquiry is factual and expression-specific.

## What it does not establish

- That static or dynamic linking, APIs, aggregation, or all plug-ins create a
  derivative or covered work.
