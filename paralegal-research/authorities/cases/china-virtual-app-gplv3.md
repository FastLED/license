## Claims, relief, and holding

- Licence/version: GPLv3, Virtual App source code; report also describes an added commercial-use statement.
- Claims: claimant alleged four applications copied substantially similar sandbox functionality, did not release source, and charged users; requested cessation and CNY15m plus CNY150k expenses.
- Awarded relief: reported order stopped download, installation, and operation of the infringing sandbox-source functionality and awarded CNY500,000 total economic loss/reasonable expenses.
- Holding reported: GPLv3 was a standard-form copyright licence; non-opening of source caused the conditional authorization to terminate, leaving use without legal/contractual permission.

## What it does not establish

- The validity of the extra commercial-use restriction, exact GPLv3 scope, an order to publish source, or a general binding Supreme Court rule.

## Key facts and reasoning

The accessible report describes a GPLv3-derived Android application and treats
the consequence of noncompliance as loss of authorization, followed by a
prohibitory/cash remedy rather than an affirmative source-release decree.
