# AUTH-0095: *Fourth Estate Public Benefit Corp. v. Wall-Street.com, LLC*

## Citation and source

- 586 U.S. 296 (2019). [Official U.S. Reports volume](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/boundvolumes/586bv.pdf).
- Later history/current status: current Supreme Court precedent checked 2026-08-25.

## Procedural posture and weight

Merits decision construing section 411(a); binding nationwide.

## Claims and requested relief

Copyright owner sued for infringement after filing applications but before the Copyright Office acted. The case did not involve an open-source license.

## Holdings and relief actually awarded

The Court held that registration occurs, for section 411(a), when the Copyright Office registers a copyright after examination or refuses registration; filing an application alone does not suffice under the ordinary rule.

## What it supports

- Keep application, registration/refusal, and filing dates separate.

## What it does not establish

- Work nationality, a section 411 exception, section 412 eligibility, or license breach.

## Key facts and reasoning

The Court read the statute's registration language and statutory scheme as adopting the registration approach.
