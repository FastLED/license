# AUTH-0074: *Montz v. Pilgrim Films & Television*

## Citation and source

- 649 F.3d 975 (9th Cir. 2011) (en banc), [official Ninth Circuit PDF](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2010/06/03/08-56954.pdf).

## Procedural posture and weight

Published en banc appeal. Binding in the Ninth Circuit on its California
implied-contract preemption holding.

## Holdings and relief actually awarded

Reversing dismissal, the court held that an alleged implied promise to pay for
use of submitted ideas is an extra element and is not preempted by § 301. It
did not establish the asserted agreement after evidence or award damages.

## What it supports

- California contract claims require attention to the promise actually alleged,
  not merely the copyrighted subject matter.

## What it does not establish

- That every source-disclosure promise has an extra element, particularly when
  the requested performance is indistinguishable from an exclusive right.
