# AUTH-0101: *Practice Management Information Corp. v. American Medical Ass'n*

## Citation and source

- 121 F.3d 516 (9th Cir. 1997). [Opinion reproduction](https://law.resource.org/pub/us/case/reporter/F3/121/121.F3d.516.94-56774.html).
- Later history/current status: no overruling authority located in this verification pass.

## Procedural posture and weight

Published Ninth Circuit merits decision; binding in that circuit and persuasive elsewhere.

## Claims and requested relief

Publisher challenged enforcement of the AMA's copyright in medical procedure codes and asserted copyright misuse based on the AMA's agreement with a federal agency.

## Holdings and relief actually awarded

The court held the AMA misused its copyright by conditioning a royalty-free government license on excluding competing coding systems, rendering the copyright unenforceable while the misuse continued. It remanded for further proceedings.

## What it supports

- Copyright misuse is a fact-specific defense when a holder leverages copyright beyond its lawful scope.

## What it does not establish

- That reciprocal licensing, source obligations, dual licensing, or ordinary compliance demands are misuse.
- A general state-law unclean-hands rule.

## Key facts and reasoning

The exclusivity condition in a government licensing arrangement, rather than ordinary exercise of copyright, drove the holding.
