## Claims, relief, and holding

- Licence/version: GNU GPL, reported as GPLv2-era enforcement.
- Claims/trigger: Sitecom sought to challenge consequences of GPL terms in a router/software distribution dispute involving Welte.
- Requested versus awarded relief: exact prayers and operative order require primary-copy retrieval; reported holding rejects GPL invalidity and recognizes licence loss on noncompliant distribution.
- Holding: the GPL's automatic loss/reversion mechanism was not an unreasonable disadvantage under German law, in the reported decision.
