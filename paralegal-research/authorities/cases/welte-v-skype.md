# AUTH-0056: *Harald Welte v Skype Technologies S.A.*

## Citation and source

- Full citation: LG München I, 12 July 2007, 7 O 5245/07, reported at CR 2008, 57.
- Direct primary source: official court PDF not located; [case metadata, excerpts, and archive link](https://dejure.org/2007,19069); [ifrOSS notice](https://ifross.org/?q=en/artikel/judgment-against-skype-final) identifies the decision as final.
- Later history: ifrOSS reports finality; no appellate judgment text located.

## Procedural posture and weight

German regional-court decision in a GPL enforcement dispute. Original language
German; no official English translation located. The English summary is a
researcher paraphrase only, and the incomplete public copy limits confidence.

## Claims, relief, and holding

- Licence/version: GNU GPL, reported GPLv2 device/telephone firmware distribution.
- Claims/trigger: alleged GPL-noncompliant distribution through a Skype-branded device/channel.
- Requested versus awarded relief: the public material confirms a judgment against Skype and identified distribution-liability issues; exact prayers and operative relief require the judgment text.
- Holding: GPL was treated as a simple licence enabling distribution only on its terms; the court rejected the asserted GPL-invalidity/right-abuse theory.

## What it does not establish

- That every brand owner is the distributor, a source-publication award, or a generally binding German result.

## Key facts and reasoning

The excerpts distinguish conduct by the actual distributor and state that a
party may distribute only under the GPL permission. The fact-specific
attribution issue is important to supply-chain claims.
