# AUTH-0099: *Kirtsaeng v. John Wiley & Sons, Inc.*

## Citation and source

- 579 U.S. 197 (2016). [Official U.S. Reports volume](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/boundvolumes/579bv.pdf).
- Later history/current status: current Supreme Court precedent checked 2026-08-25.

## Procedural posture and weight

Merits decision construing section 505; binding nationwide.

## Claims and requested relief

Prevailing defendant sought attorney fees after a copyright suit. No open-source license was involved.

## Holdings and relief actually awarded

The Court held that a court should give substantial weight to the objective reasonableness of the losing party's position while retaining discretion to consider all relevant circumstances; it vacated and remanded the fee ruling.

## What it supports

- Section 505 fees are discretionary and context-sensitive, not automatic for a prevailing party.

## What it does not establish

- Fee eligibility under section 412, a particular amount, or contractual fee shifting.

## Key facts and reasoning

The Court emphasized evenhanded treatment of prevailing plaintiffs and defendants while preserving district-court discretion.
