## License, claims, requested and actual relief
- License/version and claims: Neo4j Sweden Software License, not GPL/AGPL. Claims included copyright, DMCA, contract, and Lanham Act.
- Requested versus actual relief: Plaintiffs sought damages/injunctions. Actual relief: affirmed relevant judgment/injunction against calling ONgDB a free/open-source drop-in replacement and related false representations.

## Procedural posture and precedential weight
Unpublished Ninth Circuit memorandum; nonprecedential, persuasive only under applicable rules.

## Holding
Representation was literally false on this license and record. Does not construe GPL or award source disclosure.

## Key facts and reasoning
Section 7 permitted removing further restrictions only in stated upstream/downstream conditions, which court found absent.
