## License, claims, requested and actual relief
- License/version and claims: Creative Commons attribution license (version not confirmed from primary order in this pass). Photographer alleged uncredited web use exceeded license and included related CMI claims.
- Requested versus actual relief: Plaintiff sought statutory and actual copyright relief. Actual relief: cross-summary-judgment ruling left fair-use/license issues for trial on the identified facts and resolved some defenses; no source/publication remedy.

## Procedural posture and precedential weight

## Holding
Attribution and license compliance presented factual/contractual issues on this record; the fair-use analysis did not decide a GPL-style source condition.

## Key facts and reasoning
Photos were available through Wikimedia Commons subject to attribution; defendant later changed attribution after notice.
