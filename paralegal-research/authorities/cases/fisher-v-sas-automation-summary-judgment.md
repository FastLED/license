## License, claims, requested and actual relief
- License/version and claims: GNU GPLv3 defense concerning OpenCart, alleged covered works (MySAS and CAD Download Software), conveyance, and claimed cure. Underlying claims included copyright, trade secret, contract, and DMCA.
- Requested versus actual relief: Plaintiffs sought summary judgment on liability and defendants sought summary judgment. Actual relief: plaintiffs’ motion denied on specified license/termination claims; factual disputes remained; no source order or final damages award.

## Procedural posture and precedential weight
District-court summary-judgment order; persuasive only.

## Holding
A jury could find GPL-covered works were conveyed, giving SAS automatic GPL permission, and could find curative action began within 30 days. Court did not decide those facts or enter final GPL merits judgment.

## Key facts and reasoning
The business sale/license-back left source code on SAS servers; GPLv3 §§10 and termination/cure language were central.
