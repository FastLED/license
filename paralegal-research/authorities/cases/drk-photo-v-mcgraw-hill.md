# AUTH-0076: *DRK Photo v. McGraw-Hill Global Education Holdings*

## Citation and source

- 870 F.3d 978 (9th Cir. 2017), [official Ninth Circuit PDF](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2017/09/12/15-15106.pdf).

## Procedural posture and weight

Published Ninth Circuit merits appeal. Binding in the Ninth Circuit on the
standing principle stated, subject to the particular assignment documents.

## Holdings and relief actually awarded

The court affirmed summary judgment for the publisher because a photography
licensing agent that obtained bare assignments of accrued infringement claims,
without exclusive rights under § 106, was not the legal or beneficial owner
entitled to sue under § 501(b). No infringement relief was awarded.

## What it supports

- A purported assignee or steward needs proof it owns an exclusive right (or
  another recognized beneficial interest) at the relevant time; a claim-only
  transfer is insufficient in this circuit.

## What it does not establish

- A universal answer for every contributor agreement, collective-work owner,
  or jurisdiction outside the Ninth Circuit.
