# AUTH-0057: *AVM v Cybits*

## Citation and source

- Full citation: LG Berlin, 8 November 2011, 16 O 255/10.
- Direct primary source: [German judgment PDF](https://www.ifross.org/sites/default/files/LG%20Berlin%20AVM%20v%20Cybits_0.pdf) (court judgment reproduction); [FSFE account](https://fsfe.org/news/2011/news-20111201-02.de.html).
- Later history: the judgment itself records an earlier interim appeal, KG 6 September 2010, 24 U 71/10; later merits appeal was not located.

## Procedural posture and weight

German regional-court merits judgment. The displayed German judgment bears the
court's rubric and docket but is hosted by ifrOSS rather than an official court
portal. No official English translation; English statements are limited
researcher paraphrase.

## Claims, relief, and holding

- Licence/version: GNU GPL v2 components in AVM Fritz!Box firmware.
- Claims: AVM sought copyright, trademark, and competition-law injunctions against Cybits' Surf-Sitter DSL firmware modifier.
- Requested versus awarded relief: copyright-based main injunctions were rejected; a narrow competition-law injunction concerning misleading interface indications was granted. Costs were allocated 80% to AVM and 20% to Cybits.
- Holding: GPL components could be copied/modified under the GPL; AVM's asserted copyright theory did not prohibit the challenged modification on the record.

## What it does not establish

- That every firmware is one GPL-covered whole, or that Cybits could disregard non-GPL rights.
- A GPL owner enforcement judgment or source-disclosure remedy.

## Key facts and reasoning

Surf-Sitter altered Fritz!Box firmware containing Linux/GPL components. The
court discussed GPL copyleft while independently granting narrow relief for
misleading user-interface behavior.
