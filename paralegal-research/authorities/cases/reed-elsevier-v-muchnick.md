# AUTH-0096: *Reed Elsevier, Inc. v. Muchnick*

## Citation and source

- 559 U.S. 154 (2010). [Official U.S. Reports volume](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/boundvolumes/559bv.pdf).
- Later history/current status: current Supreme Court precedent checked 2026-08-25.

## Procedural posture and weight

Merits decision, binding nationwide.

## Claims and requested relief

Freelance authors objected to a copyright class-action settlement involving registered and unregistered works. The case did not concern reciprocal-license enforcement.

## Holdings and relief actually awarded

The Court held that section 411(a)'s registration requirement is a precondition to filing a claim, not a restriction on subject-matter jurisdiction. It remanded for further proceedings.

## What it supports

- Avoid calling section 411(a) jurisdictional.

## What it does not establish

- That a claimant can ignore section 411(a), or that section 412 or limitations is satisfied.

## Key facts and reasoning

The Court distinguished a mandatory claim-processing-type precondition from Congress's grant of federal jurisdiction.
