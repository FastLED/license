# AUTH-0034: *XimpleWare, Inc. v. Versata Software, Inc.*

## Citation and source
- No. 5:13-cv-05161-PSG, Doc. 85 (N.D. Cal. May 16, 2014). [GovInfo PDF](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-cand-5_13-cv-05161/pdf/USCOURTS-cand-5_13-cv-05161-1.pdf).
- Later history/current status: Later Doc. 142 is AUTH-0035; final merits disposition not located.

## License, claims, requested and actual relief
- License/version and claims: GNU GPLv2. Plaintiff alleged copyright and related claims against Versata and customers for VTD-XML distribution/use without GPL compliance.
- Requested versus actual relief: Plaintiff requested infringement remedies and declaratory relief. Actual relief: dismissal motions granted in part; no injunction, damages, or source disclosure awarded.

## Procedural posture and precedential weight
Pleading-stage district-court order; persuasive only.

## Holding
Alleged source-code distribution could breach GPL sufficiently to make use unlicensed, but distribution by one customer did not establish distribution by every customer. No infringement finding.

## Key facts and reasoning
Order describes GPLv2 notices, derived-code licensing, and source obligations before separating allegations by customer.
