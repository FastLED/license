# AUTH-0047: *Great Minds v. FedEx Office & Print Services, Inc.*

## Citation and source
- 886 F.3d 91 (2d Cir. 2018). [Published opinion](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca2/17-808/17-808-2018-03-21.html).
- Later history/current status: Comparable public-license decision, not GPL/AGPL authority. Recheck if different CC version or independent commercial use.

## License, claims, requested and actual relief
- License/version and claims: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0. Copyright claim alleged commercial copy-shop reproduction exceeded school districts’ noncommercial license.
- Requested versus actual relief: Plaintiff sought infringement remedies. Actual relief: dismissal affirmed; no damages, injunction, or attribution/source remedy.

## Procedural posture and precedential weight
Published Second Circuit decision; binding in circuit on construction of this license and agency point.

## Holding
Absent clear contrary language, noncommercial licensees could use commercial reproduction agents to exercise their permitted rights; FedEx was an agent of school districts.

## Key facts and reasoning
School districts ordered copies for noncommercial educational use; FedEx reproduced materials commercially for them.
