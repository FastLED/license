# AUTH-0061: OfficeTen/OpenWRT software copyright case

## Citation and source

- Full citation: Supreme People's Court, (2021) 最高法知民终51号, 12 October 2023.
- Direct primary source: [SPC IP Tribunal official Chinese case summary](https://ipc.court.gov.cn/zh-cn/news/view-3773.html).
- Later history: official summary describes the judgment as effective; no full public judgment text was retrieved.

## Procedural posture and weight

An official Supreme People's Court IP Tribunal case-summary record of a final
appeal, not the full judgment. Original language Simplified Chinese. No official
English translation located; English is a researcher paraphrase. Parties are
anonymised in the official source.

## Claims, relief, and holding

- Licence/version: GPLv2/OpenWRT in underlying software; OfficeTen gateway system.
- Claims: OfficeTen developer alleged unauthorized copying/modification/distribution; defendants argued the developer's alleged GPLv2 noncompliance meant no infringement.
- Requested versus awarded relief: first instance ordered cessation, CNY500,000 economic loss/reasonable expenses, and a website notice for at least 15 days; appeal dismissed and judgment affirmed.
- Holding: where upstream OpenWRT right holders were absent, alleged GPLv2 breach and the developer's own copyright in original contributions were distinct; GPL defence did not defeat the developer's infringement claim on this record.

## What it does not establish

- Whether the developer complied with GPLv2, whether its software was covered as a whole, or an upstream owner's GPL remedy.
- A general Chinese rule that proprietary additions never fall within GPL scope.

## Key facts and reasoning

The court emphasized contractual privity, technical separation/scope questions,
and lack of proof that the plaintiff relinquished copyright. It expressly left
potential GPL breach claims for an appropriate action.
