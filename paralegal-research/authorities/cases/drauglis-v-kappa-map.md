- Later history/current status: Verify official PDF/citation before relying; comparable CC construction only.

## License, claims, requested and actual relief
- License/version and claims: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0. Photographer alleged atlas cover credit failed license attribution requirements.
- Requested versus actual relief: Plaintiff sought copyright remedies. Actual relief: summary judgment for defendant on the license/attribution record; no damages, injunction, or source remedy.

## Procedural posture and precedential weight
District-court memorandum opinion; persuasive only.

## Holding
Court construed the credit as reasonable to the medium and sufficient on its facts. It does not decide GPL source obligations or all CC attribution disputes.

## Key facts and reasoning
Atlas used a licensed photograph on its cover with credit on back cover; court compared the credit to credits for similar map elements.
