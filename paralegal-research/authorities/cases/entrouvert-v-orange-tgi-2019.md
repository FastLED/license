# AUTH-0051: *Entr'Ouvert v Orange* — Paris first instance

## Citation and source

- Full citation: TGI Paris, 3e ch., 3e sect., 21 June 2019 (public database entry; docket not independently confirmed).
- Direct primary source: no official court-hosted full text located. The [rights-holder's French account](https://www.entrouvert.com/actualites/2019/entrouvert-versus-orange/) quotes and describes the judgment; [commercial database index](https://www.doctrine.fr/d/TGI/Paris/2019/KFV8D8364E64F34EA27A70E) is a lead only.
- Later history: Paris appeal 19 March 2021 was partially quashed by Cass. 1re civ., 5 October 2022, No. 21-15.386; remand appeal decided 14 February 2024.

## Procedural posture and weight

First-instance French judgment. It is included to prevent later history from
being mistaken for the initial outcome. Original language French; no certified
or official English translation located. English characterization is a limited
researcher paraphrase of the identified French sources.

## Claims, relief, and holding

- Licence/version: GNU GPL v2, Lasso identity-management library.
- Claims: Entr'Ouvert alleged Orange's IDMP distribution for the French government breached GPL terms, copyright, and parasitism.
- Requested versus awarded relief: claimant sought an infringement prohibition and damages; publicly accessible sources describe the copyright claim as inadmissible under contractual characterization. Exact operative relief was not independently verified.
- Holding: the reported rationale treated GPL use as contractual, triggering the non-cumulation issue under then-applied French reasoning.

## What it does not establish

- A current French rule barring copyright claims following GPL breach: its material rationale was displaced by later appellate/cassation history.
- Any source-publication order or commercial-licence award.

## Key facts and reasoning

Lasso was offered under GPLv2 or commercial terms. The first-instance decision
was a procedural turning point rather than a reliable final statement of
French GPL remedies.
