# AUTH-0092: *Winter v. Natural Resources Defense Council, Inc.*

## Citation and source

- 555 U.S. 7 (2008). [Official U.S. Reports volume](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/boundvolumes/555bv.pdf).
- Later history/current status: current Supreme Court precedent checked 2026-08-25.

## Procedural posture and weight

Merits review of a preliminary injunction; binding federal equitable precedent.

## Claims and requested relief

Environmental plaintiffs sought preliminary injunctive relief against Navy sonar-training operations. No copyright or license claim was at issue.

## Holdings and relief actually awarded

The Court held that plaintiffs seeking a preliminary injunction must establish likely irreparable harm, not merely a possibility, and applied the ordinary preliminary-relief factors. It reversed the injunction entered below.

## What it supports

- Interim source, publication, or stop-use relief requires the preliminary-injunction showing.
- Emergency requested relief is not proof that a court should award it.

## What it does not establish

- That a source-code obligation is enforceable or that any license breach causes irreparable harm.

## Key facts and reasoning

The Court balanced environmental concerns and military interests under the traditional equitable framework.
