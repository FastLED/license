# AUTH-0130: Artistic License 1.0

## Citation and source

- [OSI Artistic-1.0 record and text](https://opensource.org/license/Artistic-1.0); [OSI approved-license registry](https://opensource.org/licenses).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary historical license text. OSI's current registry lists Artistic-1.0 as approved and superseded.

## What it supports

- It permits copying, modification, distribution, and fees, but conditions modified distribution on stated alternatives, including making standard-version patches freely available or otherwise arranging access.
- The text uses Package/Standard Version/Modified Version concepts and allows aggregation; it does not create a network-use trigger, fixed source-offer term, patent grant, cure period, or forum/law clause.
- *Jacobsen* is material condition-of-license authority concerning an Artistic License, but its card is the source for the holding; this text card does not establish the decision construed every Artistic-1.0 formulation.

## What it does not establish

- A work-boundary answer, a public-source remedy, or the exact version used in a particular Artistic-labelled project.
