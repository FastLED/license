# AUTH-0022: Commons Clause License Condition v1.0

## Citation and source

- [Official Commons Clause text and FAQ](https://commonsclause.com/).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary condition text. It is an add-on condition to an identified underlying license, not a complete standalone license, and its publisher says the combined terms are not open source.

## What it supports

- It removes the right to “Sell” the Software, defined by a product/service whose value derives entirely or substantially from software functionality, while otherwise preserving the underlying license’s permissions.
- It has no independent source-disclosure, cure, patent, compatibility, forum/law, or duration rule; those depend on the underlying license and implementation.

## What it does not establish

- A complete permission grant, fixed commercial alternative, or a universal answer to whether an offering is “Sell.”

## Key facts and reasoning

The required notice identifies the software, underlying license, and licensor. Analysis must read the combined instrument, including any patent/termination/forum terms below it.
