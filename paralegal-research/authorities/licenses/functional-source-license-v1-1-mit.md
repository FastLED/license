# AUTH-0024: Functional Source License 1.1 (MIT future license)

## Citation and source

- [Official FSL site and FSL-1.1-MIT raw text](https://fsl.software/); [Fair Source definition](https://fair.io/about/).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary publisher materials. Before conversion, FSL is Fair Source/source-available, not an OSI-approved license; the designated future MIT license is separately OSI-approved.

## What it supports

- FSL provides source access and permits specified use while prohibiting “Competing Use”; each release converts after the stated two-year period to MIT in the FSL-1.1-MIT variant.
- It is not reciprocal source disclosure; cure/reinstatement, patents, forum/law, and commercial-alternative details must be verified against the exact raw version/release notice.

## What it does not establish

- That all Fair Source licenses have a two-year conversion or identical competitive-use boundary.

## Key facts and reasoning

The official site characterizes FSL as conversion to MIT or Apache-2.0 after two years. Deployment and product facts determine whether the competition limitation applies.
