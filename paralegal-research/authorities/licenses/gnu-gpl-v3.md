# AUTH-0010: GNU General Public License version 3

## Citation and source

- [Official FSF GPL-3.0 text](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html); [OSI approved-license registry](https://opensource.org/licenses).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary license text. OSI lists GPL-3.0 as approved.

## What it supports

- Conveying a covered work/object code triggers corresponding-source obligations (ss. 4--6); a source offer under s. 6(b) must remain valid three years.
- Section 8 supplies automatic termination plus provisional and specified permanent reinstatement; ss. 11 and 10 provide patent provisions and anti-further-restrictions terms.
- The license has no selected forum/law and permits separate permissions/commercial alternatives outside its grant.

## What it does not establish

- A network-use trigger (unlike AGPL s. 13), outcome for a particular aggregate, or court remedy.

## Key facts and reasoning

The covered boundary is the Program and works “based on” it; s. 5 permits aggregation on storage/distribution media where copyright does not extend to the aggregate. Compatibility needs version- and combination-specific analysis.
