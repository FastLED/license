# AUTH-0021: Elastic License 2.0

## Citation and source

- [Official Elastic ELv2 text and FAQ](https://www.elastic.co/licensing/elastic-license/faq); [official Elastic repository license text](https://github.com/elastic/elasticsearch/blob/main/licenses/ELASTIC-LICENSE-2.0.txt).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary licensor materials. ELv2 is source-available and not listed in OSI’s approved registry.

## What it supports

- It permits use, copying, distribution, and preparation of derivative works, subject to three restrictions: no providing the software to third parties as a hosted/managed service, no circumventing license-key functionality, and no removal/obscuring notices.
- It contains no reciprocal public-source disclosure duty, patent grant, compatibility rule, cure/reinstatement mechanism, or forum/law selection in the standard text.

## What it does not establish

- That a particular SaaS integration provides “the software” as a managed service, or that project-level multi-licensing changes ELv2’s status.

## Key facts and reasoning

Commercial permission can be obtained separately; Elastic’s FAQ must not replace the operative text for an ambiguous deployment.
