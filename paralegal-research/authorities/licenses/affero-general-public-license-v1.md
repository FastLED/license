# AUTH-0132: Affero General Public License version 1

## Citation and source

- [Original Affero AGPL v1 text](http://www.affero.org/oagpl.html); [SPDX identifier record](https://spdx.org/licenses/AGPL-1.0-or-later.html).

## Procedural posture and weight

Historical licensor text: Affero's 2002 modification of GPL-2.0. It is distinct from GNU AGPL-3.0 and is not an entry in the current OSI approved-license registry.

## What it supports

- GPLv2-style distribution/source and automatic-termination rules apply, with added section 2(d): a modified program normally used over a network must offer complete machine-readable source to each user through the network.
- The network rule is tied to a modified version and ordinary interactive network use; it is not GNU AGPL-3.0 section 13 and has no GPLv3-style reinstatement rule.
- No separate patent grant, fixed general cure, or forum/law selection appears in the historical text.

## What it does not establish

- The meaning of the historical network interaction in a current deployment, its compatibility with AGPL-3.0, or any remedy.
