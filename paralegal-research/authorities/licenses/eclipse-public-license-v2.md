# AUTH-0014: Eclipse Public License 2.0

## Citation and source

- [Official Eclipse EPL-2.0 text](https://www.eclipse.org/legal/epl-2.0/); [OSI approved-license registry](https://opensource.org/licenses).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary agreement text. Eclipse Foundation is initial Agreement Steward; OSI lists EPL-2.0 as approved.

## What it supports

- Distribution triggers Program source availability and notice (s. 3); Modified Works exclude source files containing only declarations/interfaces/types/classes solely to link, bind, or subclass.
- Material noncompliance terminates rights after a reasonable cure period following awareness; contributor copyright/patent grants are in s. 2.
- No forum/law selection; s. 3.3 permits a GPL secondary license if the initial contributor so designates.

## What it does not establish

- A universal linking answer, a fixed cure duration, or judicial remedies.

## Key facts and reasoning

Source must be available in customary exchange media with reasonable access information. Commercial alternatives are not restricted by the text.
