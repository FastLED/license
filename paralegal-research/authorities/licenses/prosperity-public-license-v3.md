# AUTH-0135: Prosperity Public License 3.0.0

## Citation and source

- [Publisher-maintained 3.0.0 text](https://github.com/licensezero/prosperitylicense.com/blob/main/3.0.0.md); [publisher repository](https://github.com/licensezero/prosperity-public-license).

## Procedural posture and weight

Authenticated publisher repository text. It is a source-available commercial-use restriction, not in the OSI approved registry.

## What it supports

- Noncommercial use and sharing are free, while commercial use is limited to one 30-day trial per company rather than a recurring per-person trial.
- It requires notices on copies, exempts enumerated personal/noncommercial organization uses, and treats qualifying contributed-back work as noncommercial for the stated purpose.
- It supplies copyright and patent grants with a patent-claim defense and a 30-day notices excuse; it does not impose reciprocal source disclosure, delayed conversion, or select forum/law.

## What it does not establish

- Whether a particular activity is commercial, the availability or price of a commercial license, or a judicial remedy.
