# AUTH-0134: Parity Public License 7.0.0

## Citation and source

- [Official Parity 7.0.0 text](https://paritylicense.com/versions/7.0.0.html); [version index](https://paritylicense.com/versions).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary publisher text (Artless Devices). It is a non-OSI-listed public reciprocal license, not an OSI-approved instrument.

## What it supports

- Its broad copyleft rule reaches software developed, operated, or analyzed with the licensed software, subject to the defined prototype exception.
- Covered software must be published in preferred source form through a freely accessible, widely used distribution system within 30 days; parts must be under Parity or a license allowing everything Parity allows.
- It has 30-day knowledge-based excuses for Copyleft/Notices, copyright and patent grants with patent-claim defense, but no forum/law selection.

## What it does not establish

- Whether its broad "with this software" rule reaches a given service, the validity of its condition/contract characterization, or a remedy.
