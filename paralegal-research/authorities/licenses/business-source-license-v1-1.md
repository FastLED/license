# AUTH-0020: Business Source License 1.1

## Citation and source

- [Official MariaDB BUSL-1.1 text](https://mariadb.com/bsl11/).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary steward text. It expressly says it is not an Open Source license; it is not in OSI’s approved registry.

## What it supports

- Source may be public, but production use is limited by the per-release Additional Use Grant; all copies/derivatives remain subject to the license before conversion.
- On the Change Date or fourth anniversary, whichever comes first, the stated Change License applies; noncompliant use requires a commercial license or refraining.
- No patent grant, cure, compatibility rule, or forum/law selection is stated in the standard form.

## What it does not establish

- A fixed production restriction, Change Date, or Change License without the release-specific parameters.

## Key facts and reasoning

The standard text is a template whose key commercial and timing terms are supplied per Licensed Work; it is not a copyleft source-disclosure regime.
