# AUTH-0018: Reciprocal Public License 1.5

## Citation and source

- [Official OSI RPL-1.5 text](https://opensource.org/license/RPL-1.5); [OSI approved-license registry](https://opensource.org/licenses).

## Procedural posture and weight

OSI official text and approved-license record.

## What it supports

- “Deploy” includes internal or external use/distribution; deployment triggers public availability of source for the licensed software and extensions (ss. 1.12, 3.2, 6.1).
- Section 8 gives a 30-day cure after notice for a first violation and immediate termination otherwise; ss. 3 and 10 contain patent terms/retaliation.
- Section 13 selects California law and San Francisco courts; separate commercial licensing is not forbidden.

## What it does not establish

- Every component that is a Required Component, a court’s treatment of internal deployment, or compatibility.

## Key facts and reasoning

The text’s deployment and Required Components definitions create a broad reciprocal boundary; the source audience is the open-source community at large.
