# AUTH-0143: CockroachDB Community License Agreement

## Citation and source

- [Official CockroachDB Community License Agreement](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/cockroachdb-community-license/); [CockroachDB licensing documentation](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/stable/licensing).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary current Cockroach Labs agreement. It is a product-level agreement, not an OSI-approved public license; the current agreement expressly says the Core version's applicable license is found in the release's license file and that most agreement terms do not apply to Core.

## What it supports

- For CockroachDB Self-hosted, it grants limited business-purpose copyright/patent rights, while paid-enterprise terms/license-key status govern designated Enterprise features; the agreement also supplies a time-limited internal evaluation license.
- Redistribution of a Work/Derivative Work carries license, modification, and notice requirements, but the agreement itself does not impose reciprocal public-source disclosure.
- It has patent-litigation termination, New York/U.S. governing law, and a prevailing-party fees provision; the release-specific Core text, not this agreement, controls a Core open/source-available classification.

## What it does not establish

- The license of a particular CockroachDB Core release, a historical BUSL/CCL status, enterprise-feature entitlement, or enforcement outcome.
