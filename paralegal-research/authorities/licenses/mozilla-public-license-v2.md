# AUTH-0013: Mozilla Public License 2.0

## Citation and source

- [Official Mozilla MPL-2.0 text](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/MPL/2.0/); [OSI approved-license registry](https://opensource.org/licenses).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary license text; Mozilla is steward and OSI lists MPL-2.0 as approved.

## What it supports

- Distribution of Covered Software source/executable form triggers ss. 3.1--3.2 source availability; “Modifications” are file-level, while a Larger Work may include separate non-covered files (s. 1.7, 1.10, 3.3).
- Section 5 has 30/60-day cure/reinstatement rules; s. 2 grants contributor patents and s. 5.2 has patent-litigation termination.
- Section 8 selects defendant-principal-place forum/law; ss. 3.3 and 10.3 address larger works and selected GPL-family secondary licensing.

## What it does not establish

- That a particular file, combination, or modified FastLED text has MPL’s terms or compatibility.

## Key facts and reasoning

Source availability is to executable recipients by reasonable means in a timely manner. Commercial licensing is not prohibited; separate grants remain possible.
