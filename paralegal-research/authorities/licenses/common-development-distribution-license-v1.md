# AUTH-0015: Common Development and Distribution License 1.0

## Citation and source

- [Official Oracle CDDL-1.0 text](https://docs.oracle.com/en/servers/x86/x9-2/license-manual/common-development-and-distribution-license-cddl.html); [OSI approved-license registry](https://opensource.org/licenses).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary license text as published by Oracle; OSI lists CDDL-1.0 as approved.

## What it supports

- Distribution of Executable requires access to Covered Software source (ss. 3.1--3.2); covered boundary is original/modification files, while a Larger Work may include other code.
- Section 6 terminates on breach if not cured within 30 days after notice; s. 2.1 grants copyright and patent rights with stated limits and s. 6.2 terminates patent rights for patent action.
- Section 9.3 selects California law and Santa Clara County venue; the text allows different licenses for executable larger works subject to CDDL compliance.

## What it does not establish

- Compatibility with GPL or an outcome for a particular file boundary.

## Key facts and reasoning

The source obligation attaches on distribution, not private deployment. “Commercial Use” is expressly permitted.
