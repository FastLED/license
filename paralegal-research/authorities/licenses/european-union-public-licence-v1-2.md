# AUTH-0016: European Union Public Licence 1.2

## Citation and source

- [European Commission/Joinup EUPL-1.2 text](https://joinup.ec.europa.eu/collection/eupl/eupl-text-eupl-12); [OSI approved-license registry](https://opensource.org/licenses).

## Procedural posture and weight

Official European Commission license text; OSI lists EUPL-1.2 as approved.

## What it supports

- Distribution or communication of derivatives triggers same-license obligations (arts. 5(c), 5(e)); source is defined as the preferred human-readable form.
- Article 12 terminates automatically on breach and provides cure after notice within 30 days; art. 2 includes contributor patent rights, art. 13 ends them for patent litigation.
- Article 15 selects EU member-state law and competent court based on licensor residence/seat; art. 5 permits listed compatible licenses for derivative distribution.

## What it does not establish

- The applicable copyright law’s derivative-work conclusion, exact compatibility in a fact pattern, or a remedy.

## Key facts and reasoning

The license is reciprocal and distribution/communication focused. Its named compatibility list is textual, not a blanket interoperability opinion.
