# AUTH-0012: GNU Lesser General Public License version 3

## Citation and source

- [Official FSF LGPL-3.0 text](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.html); [OSI approved-license registry](https://opensource.org/licenses).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary license text; OSI lists LGPL-3.0 as approved.

## What it supports

- LGPL-3.0 adds permissions to GPL-3.0: a combined work may be conveyed under chosen terms if ss. 4--5 conditions preserve the LGPL-covered library and users’ modification/reverse-engineering/relinking rights.
- GPL-3.0 s. 8 termination/reinstatement and s. 11 patent provisions apply through the incorporated GPL terms.

## What it does not establish

- Whether a particular combined work is compliant or whether a court will adopt an asserted linkage theory.

## Key facts and reasoning

The license’s defined “Application,” “Combined Work,” and “Minimal Corresponding Source” boundaries matter. It has no independent forum/law choice and permits commercial distribution.
