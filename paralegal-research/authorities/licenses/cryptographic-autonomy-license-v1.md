# AUTH-0133: Cryptographic Autonomy License 1.0

## Citation and source

- [OSI CAL-1.0 text and approval record](https://opensource.org/license/CAL-1.0); [OSI registry](https://opensource.org/licenses).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary license text. OSI records CAL-1.0 as approved; the steward link is Process Mechanics.

## What it supports

- Private use/modification is broadly permitted; providing any part, aspect, or element to a non-affiliated third party triggers autonomy-preserving source, data, and capability obligations.
- The terms expressly characterize rules as contractual obligations and license conditions and identify the licensor and a Recipient as intended third-party beneficiaries who may seek specific performance.
- It includes patent terms and termination mechanics, but no selected forum/law in the standard text.

## What it does not establish

- That any recipient has standing or will obtain specific performance in a particular jurisdiction, or that a deployment fact meets the third-party trigger.
