# AUTH-0137: Jelurida Public License 1.1

## Citation and source

- [Official Jelurida JPL v1.1 text (PDF)](https://www.jelurida.com/sites/default/files/JPLv1.1-NRS.pdf).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary licensor text for a distributed-ledger software license. It is project-specific and not listed in OSI's approved registry.

## What it supports

- Conveying a Covered Work requires notices; modified/distributed covered works must be licensed as a whole at no charge under JPL, subject to separate-work and aggregation language.
- Object-code conveyance requires Corresponding Source by one of stated methods, including a three-year written offer; breach automatically terminates rights, without a general cure rule.
- JPL adds DLT-specific anti-fork/airdrop requirements in Special Conditions and identifies a separate commercial-license/permission route for certain DLT uses; the exact Special Conditions control that route.

## What it does not establish

- The application of any airdrop percentage or Special Condition to a particular chain, or a remedy outside the existing case's interim scope.
