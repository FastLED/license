# AUTH-0140: Sustainable Use License 1.0

## Citation and source

- [n8n's official Sustainable Use License page and text](https://docs.n8n.io/privacy-and-security/sustainable-use-license); [n8n documentation source](https://github.com/n8n-io/n8n-docs/blob/main/docs/privacy-and-security/sustainable-use-license.md).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary project text for n8n's fair-code instrument. It is source-available, not OSI-approved open source; "Sustainable Use License" is a name used by more than one project, so this card is limited to the authenticated n8n v1 text.

## What it supports

- Use/modification is free for internal business, noncommercial, or personal use; distribution/provision to others is permitted only free of charge for noncommercial purposes.
- It requires retaining notices and leaves out a reciprocal source-publication duty, delayed conversion, stated patent grant, general cure, and selected forum/law.
- n8n identifies a separate enterprise/commercial agreement for uses outside the grant.

## What it does not establish

- The terms of another project's similarly titled license, whether an integration is internal business use, or commercial-license availability/price.
