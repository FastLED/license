# AUTH-0131: Artistic License 2.0

## Citation and source

- [OSI Artistic-2.0 text and approval record](https://opensource.org/license/Artistic-2.0); [OSI registry](https://opensource.org/licenses).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary steward/OSI text. OSI identifies the Perl and Raku Foundation as steward and records approval in 2007.

## What it supports

- Private use and modification are unrestricted; modified-source distribution must document differences and satisfy one of the section 4 alternatives, while compiled distribution has its own source-instructions rule.
- Compiled Standard Versions may be distributed without source if valid source instructions are included; invalid instructions require replacement or cessation, with a 30-day awareness window that avoids forfeiture.
- It has an express contributor patent grant and patent-litigation termination, permits distributor fees, and has no network trigger or forum/law selection.

## What it does not establish

- Whether a package exposes a direct interface under section 8, compatibility in a concrete combination, or a judicial remedy.
