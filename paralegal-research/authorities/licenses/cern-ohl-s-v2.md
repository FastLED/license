# AUTH-0139: CERN-OHL-S-2.0

## Citation and source

- [CERN OHL home and definitive-text links](https://cern-ohl.web.cern.ch/home); [OSI CERN-OHL-S-2.0 text/approval](https://opensource.org/license/CERN-OHL-S-2.0).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary CERN/OSI text. OSI approves this strongly reciprocal instrument, but it is an open-hardware/design-source license rather than a software-license comparator.

## What it supports

- Modified Covered Source and Products require Complete Source or source-location notice to recipients; the location must be reasonably accessible for at least three years.
- "Source" includes design material/digital code needed to make or test a Product, and strong reciprocity reaches a larger work incorporating Covered Source, excluding Available Components as defined.
- It has no commercial-use restriction, selected forum/law, or general cure/reinstatement provision in the standard text.

## What it does not establish

- Software source obligations, a product-safety result, or a remedy for hardware conveyance.
