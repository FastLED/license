# AUTH-0142: Confluent Community License 1.0

## Citation and source

- [Official Confluent Community License 1.0 text](https://www.confluent.io/confluent-community-license); [Confluent FAQ](https://www.confluent.io/confluent-community-license-faq/).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary licensor agreement. Confluent's FAQ distinguishes source access from open-source status; this instrument is not listed in OSI's approved registry.

## What it supports

- It grants use, modification, distribution, and copying, but excludes operating a SaaS/PaaS/IaaS or similar online service that competes with Confluent services providing the software.
- Distribution requires modification and proprietary notices; recipients get their own license only on agreement to the same terms, and no sublicensing is granted.
- Breach causes automatic permanent termination. The text has no reciprocal source duty, patent grant, or cure; it chooses California law, Santa Clara courts for U.S. licensees, and Palo Alto JAMS arbitration for others.

## What it does not establish

- Whether a given online service competes, whether a project component is licensed under this rather than Apache 2.0, or an enforcement outcome.
