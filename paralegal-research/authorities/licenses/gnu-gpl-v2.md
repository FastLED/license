# AUTH-0009: GNU General Public License version 2

## Citation and source

- [Official FSF GPL-2.0 text](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/gpl-2.0.html); [OSI approved-license registry](https://opensource.org/licenses).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary license text, not legislation or an interpretation. OSI lists GPL-2.0 as approved.

## What it supports

- Distribution of modified work or executable/object code triggers whole-work GPL source obligations (ss. 2--3); private use is outside those distribution clauses.
- Written offer option lasts at least three years (s. 3(b)); breach automatically terminates permission (s. 4); no express cure/reinstatement mechanism.
- It permits charging fees and separate commercial licensing, has no express patent grant, and supplies no forum/law clause.

## What it does not establish

- The copyright boundary of a particular combined work, a remedy, or a universal compatibility conclusion.

## Key facts and reasoning

The textual boundary is a work “based on the Program” / whole work containing portions of the Program. The FSF text is the operative source; [existing U.S. cards](../cases/jacobsen-v-katzer.md) address limited litigation doctrine, not GPL-2.0 as a whole.
