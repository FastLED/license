# AUTH-0011: GNU Lesser General Public License version 2.1

## Citation and source

- [Official FSF LGPL-2.1 text](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html); [OSI approved-license registry](https://opensource.org/licenses).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary license text; OSI lists LGPL-2.1 as approved.

## What it supports

- Modifying/distributing the Library triggers LGPL source terms (ss. 2--4); works using the Library may be distributed under chosen terms only if s. 6 conditions, including relinking/modification access, are met.
- Object-code offer variants include a three-year written-offer route (s. 6(a)); s. 8 terminates on breach without express cure.
- LGPL-2.1 incorporates GPL-2.0 where stated and has no express patent grant or forum/law clause.

## What it does not establish

- Whether a technical linkage satisfies a section 6 condition or what the governing copyright law calls a derivative work.

## Key facts and reasoning

The Library / work-that-uses-the-Library distinction is textual and fact dependent. It allows fee charging and separate licensing of independently written material.
