# AUTH-0023: PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0

## Citation and source

- [Official PolyForm Shield 1.0.0 text](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/shield/1.0.0); [PolyForm license family](https://polyformproject.org/licenses).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary publisher text. PolyForm describes Shield as barring competition with the provider; it is not in OSI’s approved registry.

## What it supports

- It permits use, modification, and distribution subject to a noncompete purpose limitation; the boundary is the Software and changes/new works, not a reciprocal source-disclosure rule.
- It grants a patent license, ends it on patent claims, and offers a one-time 32-day post-written-notice cure; no forum/law or compatibility clause is selected.

## What it does not establish

- Whether two products compete, a commercial-price term, or any public-source duration.

## Key facts and reasoning

The license’s “Competition” definition deliberately reaches substitute functionality across interfaces/platforms. It permits distribution but does not impose a public-source duty.
