# AUTH-0138: Sybase Open Watcom Public License 1.0

## Citation and source

- [OSI Watcom-1.0 text and approval record](https://opensource.org/license/Watcom-1.0); [OSI registry](https://opensource.org/licenses).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary OSI-hosted historical text. OSI lists Watcom-1.0 as an approved non-reusable license.

## What it supports

- "Deploy" includes non-R&D/internal organizational use as well as external use/distribution; deployed modifications trigger public source availability for deployment duration or 12 months from first deployment, whichever is longer.
- It uses covered-code/modification/larger-work concepts, grants copyright and patent rights, requires 30-day cure after awareness, and permits object-code distribution under a protective separate agreement.
- It selects California/U.S. law and Northern District of California dispute resolution.

## What it does not establish

- Whether a particular internal use is deployment, the applicable source scope, or a court remedy.
