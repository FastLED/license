# AUTH-0136: CeCILL 2.1

## Citation and source

- [Official CeCILL 2.1 English text](https://www.cecill.info/licences/Licence_CeCILL_V2.1-en.html); [CeCILL site](https://www.cecill.info/index.en.html); [OSI registry](https://opensource.org/licenses).

## Procedural posture and weight

Primary steward text. The French and English 2.1 texts are both authentic according to the steward; OSI lists CeCILL-2.1 as approved.

## What it supports

- Distribution of modified software requires source availability under CeCILL; internal and external modules receive different treatment and specified outbound compatibility includes GPL-family/EUPL choices.
- It grants patent rights subject to termination, permits commercial use and fee-bearing services, and contains French-law and Paris-court provisions.
- The text's contractual and intellectual-property framing is French-law specific; it is not a network/deployment source trigger.

## What it does not establish

- A complete linking/module classification, a universal compatibility result, or a remedy outside the selected law/forum.
