# AUTH-0017: Open Software License 3.0

## Citation and source

- [Official OSI OSL-3.0 text](https://opensource.org/license/OSL-3.0); [OSI approved-license registry](https://opensource.org/licenses).

## Procedural posture and weight

OSI’s official text and approved-license record.

## What it supports

- Distribution/communication and “External Deployment” trigger reciprocal treatment (ss. 1(c), 5); s. 3 requires source with each distributed copy or repository access while distribution continues.
- Section 9 conditions rights and terminates immediately on failure of s. 1(c); no general cure is stated. Sections 2 and 10 give patent grant and patent-action termination.
- The text has no forum/law clause and permits licensor dual licensing; it requires reasonable efforts to obtain recipient express assent on distribution/communication.

## What it does not establish

- Effectiveness of assent in a jurisdiction, a specific external-deployment fact pattern, or judicial relief.

## Key facts and reasoning

“External Deployment” reaches network availability to anyone other than the licensee, making it broader than a mere distribution trigger.
