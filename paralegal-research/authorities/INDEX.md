# Authority index

identifier, authority name, or type, then open the card before relying on a
proposition. The matter memos and [proposition map](../PROPOSITION-MAP.md)
explain how an authority may be used. A card records preliminary research for
attorney review; inclusion is not a statement that the authority controls a
particular dispute.

Identifier gaps are deliberate reservations from parallel research. Do not

## Use requirements

- Read the card's procedural posture, precedential weight, source quality,
  actual holding or text, and stated limits.
- Do not treat requested relief, allegations, defaults, settlements, or
  commentary as a contested merits holding.
- Recheck time-sensitive dockets, statutes, official license text, steward or
  OSI status, translations, and later history before giving a current answer.
- Route unresolved research through [the agent workflow](../AGENTS.md) and log
  systematic searches using [the search-log template](../templates/SEARCH-LOG.md).
