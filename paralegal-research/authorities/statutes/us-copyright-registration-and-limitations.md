# AUTH-0090: 17 U.S.C. §§ 411(a) and 507(b)

## Citation and source

- Current official text: [§411(a)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title17-section411); [§507(b)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=%28title%3A17+section%3A507+edition%3Aprelim%29).
- Later history/current status: official preliminary text checked 2026-08-25.

## Procedural posture and weight

Federal statutory text, binding subject to controlling construction and applicable exceptions.

## Claims and requested relief

Section 411(a) generally restricts institution of an infringement action for a United States work until preregistration or registration, with the stated refusal route. Section 507(b) says a civil action must be commenced within three years after the claim accrued.

## Holdings and relief actually awarded

Not a judicial decision. The provisions do not themselves decide registration validity, work nationality, accrual doctrine, claim ownership, or any remedy award.

## What it supports

- Registration and limitations are separate threshold/timing questions for an infringement action.
- The statutory limitations text uses accrual, not a license-specific cure rule.

## What it does not establish

- Section 412 enhanced-remedy eligibility (covered separately by AUTH-0006).
- A state-law contract limitations period, discovery rule, or specific performance remedy.

## Key facts and reasoning

Apply the text work by work and claim by claim, then use controlling case law for what registration and accrual mean.
