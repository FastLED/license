# AUTH-0070: 17 U.S.C. §§ 101, 106, and 301

## Citation and source

- Copyright Act definitions, exclusive rights, and express-preemption rule;
  [official House Code Collection, § 301](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title17-section301&num=0&edition=prelim), with §§ 101 and 106 in the same title.
- Current statutory text, verified 2026-08-25.

## Procedural posture and weight

Federal statute. Its application depends on controlling circuit and state law;
it does not itself classify a license term as a condition or contract promise.

## Holdings and relief actually awarded

Not applicable. Section 106 enumerates exclusive copyright rights. Section
301(a) preempts specified state-law legal or equitable rights that are
equivalent to those exclusive rights in works within copyright subject matter;
§ 301(b) preserves enumerated categories including contractual rights.

## What it supports

- A copyright infringement theory needs conduct within an exclusive right, not
  merely a breached promise.
- The statutory preemption inquiry has subject-matter and equivalency
  components; contract labeling alone is not a complete answer.

## What it does not establish

- Whether a particular source, notice, network, or deployment term carries the
  necessary copyright nexus.
- Whether a particular state-law claim includes a qualitatively different
  contractual element.
