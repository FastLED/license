# Case gap audit — 2026-08-25

> Paralegal research for attorney review, not legal advice. This log records
> search coverage and source boundaries; a negative result does not establish
> that no case or docket exists.

## Scope and method

Actor: copyright owner, licensor, recipient/customer, distributor, or alleged
third-party beneficiary. Trigger: distribution, missing notices/source, asserted
source-performance right, derivative-work scope, cure, or a GPL-based defence.
Jurisdictions: U.S.; EU, France, Germany, Italy, Netherlands, UK, Spain, China,
Japan, and Australia. The audit required a direct court/docket/government source
or a materially identifiable full judgment reproduction before creating a new
card. It did not revise existing cards or license text.
