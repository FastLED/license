# International case census search log — 2026-08-25

> Research log for attorney review, not legal advice. A search hit is not a
> holding unless confirmed in the linked authority card.

## Protocol

**Inclusion.** Publicly accessible court judgments, official court case
summaries, or official EU/government repositories materially addressing an
open-source/reciprocal licence, GPL-based defence, licence loss, compliance, or
remedy. **Exclusion.** Commentary, party press releases, and commercial
database-only reports unless retained only as a lead; settlements are not
classified as decisions. Original language controls; translation provenance is
recorded on every card.
